What GradMate does

1-click resume & cover letter engine

ATS-optimized résumés and cover letters from the skills, certifications, internships, and experience already in your GradMate profile. Export a PDF when you are ready to apply.

Productivity & focus

A deadline-aware task scheduler plus a Pomodoro timer with work and break blocks — so study sessions and career admin share one list.

Peer network

In-app study-buddy chat on Firestore, with push notifications when a classmate replies. Keep homework talk off group SMS.
