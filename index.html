One place for school and career

TrackEd helps students run the day and the résumé: tasks, focus sessions, skills, internships, a shareable ID card, and a PDF resume — with Google Sign-In and Firebase.

  • Smart planner Type “Study SwiftUI at 8pm tomorrow.” Calendar view, categories, and priorities.
  • Focus + career Pomodoro sessions, projects, internships, certifications, work history, and resume export.
  • iOS, built to last SwiftUI, Core Data, Firestore chat, push notifications, light and dark mode.
